## Summary

Adds a demo-only Jarvis loop for nights when the Windows SolidWorks Workstation is offline. A request goes into a local file queue, a worker claims it, and build123d builds a 3 part assembly (base plate, L bracket, clevis pin). The output is one STEP per part, an assembly STEP, a PNG render and a geometry check report. The loop is off unless `JARVIS_DEMO_ENABLED=1`.

## Problem

We want to show "Blaine tells his Workstation to design an assembly from his iPhone" before window 2 and the Windows session. Until now there was no runnable rehearsal of the request, worker and result loop that needs neither the hosted inbox nor SolidWorks.
