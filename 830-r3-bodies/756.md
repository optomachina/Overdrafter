## Summary

Both quote-select RPCs now check `user_can_edit_job` instead of the access-only helpers:
- `api_set_job_selected_vendor_quote_offer(uuid,uuid)`: `user_can_access_job` becomes `user_can_edit_job`. It already had verified auth.
- `api_select_quote_option(uuid,uuid,text)`: adds `require_verified_auth()` first, and `user_can_access_package` becomes `user_can_edit_job(package.job_id)`.

Both are SECURITY DEFINER with `search_path = ''` (was `pg_catalog`) and fully qualified references. EXECUTE is revoked from public and anon and granted to authenticated.

## Problem

OVD-766 finding 4: these writes were gated by read-level access.
