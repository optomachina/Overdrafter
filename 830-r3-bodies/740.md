## Summary

- OVD-763 slice 1: provider session health records, refresh policy with Blaine's approved limits, and an extensible alert-channel layer (email, Slack, SMS, dashboard, each with an on/off selector).

## Problem

- Vendor sessions expire within hours and Blaine refreshes them by hand. Slice 1 adds the health model, policy and alerting seams; actual re-login comes in later slices.

## Scope

- worker/src/providerSessionHealth.ts: per-provider health from the existing probe (authenticated, logged_out, blocked, mfa_required, error), with probe and clock injected.
- worker/src/providerSessionRefreshPolicy.ts: KEEP_VERSIONS=5, MAX_LOGINS_PER_24H=3, backoff 1/5/30 min, kill switch after 3 failed cycles. MFA and captcha are never retried.
- worker/src/alerts/: AlertChannel interface; email, Slack and SMS stubs (no network) default off, dashboard default on. One alert per state change.
- No migrations, secrets, deploy or login code. Session paths are sensitive, so this waits for Blaine's OK.
